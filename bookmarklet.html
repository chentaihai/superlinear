🔍 Search Engine Switcher Bookmarklets

Instantly switch your search query to another search engine with one click!

📖 How to Install

  1. Drag any of the links below to your browser's bookmarks bar
  2. OR right-click the link and select "Bookmark Link"
  3. When on any search results page (Google, Bing, etc.), click the bookmarklet
  4. Your search will open in a new tab using the alternative search engine
